About Our Platform

Transforming Agriculture With Direct Connectivity

Krishi Connect was created with a simple mission: to give farmers total control over their sales while providing buyers with authentic, fresh-from-the-farm produce at fair market rates.

Zero Middleman Commission

Farmers retain 100% of their earnings with transparent pricing and no third-party cuts.

Instant Price & Crop Alerts

Buyers get notified as soon as their required crops are listed by nearby farmers.

Direct Buyer-Farmer Messaging

Built-in inquiry system allows direct communication, negotiations, and fulfillment.

Step-by-Step Guide

How Krishi Connect Works

Connecting hard-working farmers with buyers in 3 simple steps.

Step 1

1. Farmers List Produce

Farmers upload details about their harvested crops, specifying total quantity (kg/tons), harvest date, price per unit, and location.

Step 2

2. Buyers Explore & Inquire

Wholesalers, retailers, and individual buyers filter listings, compare prices, and send direct inquiry messages to farmers.

Step 3

3. Direct Deal & Delivery

Farmer and buyer agree on quantity and logistics directly, establishing a transparent relationship with guaranteed fresh produce.
